# RetailMart 360: Centralized Business KPI Dictionary

**Prepared by:** Senior Data Analyst & Analytics Consultant  
**Project:** RetailMart 360 — Retail Sales & Customer Intelligence  
**Scope:** Standardized Business Formulas & Metadata Reference

## 1. Commercial Performance KPIs

### 1.1. Realized Revenue (Shipped Sales Proxy)
* **Business Definition:** Net recognized commercial sales resulting from orders that have completed warehouse fulfillment and shipped to the customer.
* **Formula:** $\sum (\text{quantityOrdered} \times \text{priceEach})$ where $\text{status} = \text{'Shipped'}$.
* **Granularity:** Order-Line Item (`orderdetails`).
* **Filters / Exclusions:** Excludes `Cancelled`, `In Process`, `On Hold`, `Disputed`, and `Resolved` orders.
* **Date Basis:** `orders.orderDate` (transaction order placement date).
* **Baseline Value:** **$8,865,094.64** across 2,771 order lines.
* **Analytical Limitations:** Represents an analytical proxy for realized sales. Does not account for post-delivery commercial returns or cash accounting reconciliation.

### 1.2. Booked Revenue (All Order Statuses)
* **Business Definition:** Gross value of all commercial purchase commitments entered into the order management system regardless of fulfillment status.
* **Formula:** $\sum (\text{quantityOrdered} \times \text{priceEach})$.
* **Granularity:** Order-Line Item (`orderdetails`).
* **Filters / Exclusions:** None (covers all 6 fulfillment statuses).
* **Baseline Value:** **$9,604,190.61** across 2,996 order lines.

### 1.3. Average Order Value (AOV)
* **Business Definition:** Average dollar volume realized per completed transaction header.
* **Formula:** $\frac{\text{Total Realized Revenue}}{\text{Total Distinct Shipped Orders}} = \frac{\$8,865,094.64}{303}$.
* **Granularity:** Order Header (`orders`).
* **Baseline Value:** **$29,257.74**.

### 1.4. Total Physical Units Sold
* **Business Definition:** Aggregate quantity of scale-model vehicle units fulfilled.
* **Formula:** $\sum \text{quantityOrdered}$ where $\text{status} = \text{'Shipped'}$.
* **Baseline Value:** **99,067 units** (Booked total across all statuses: 105,166 units).

## 2. Customer Intelligence & Loyalty KPIs

### 2.1. Active Purchasing Customers
* **Business Definition:** Count of distinct customer business accounts that have placed at least 1 order in historical records.
* **Formula:** $\text{COUNT(DISTINCT orders.customerNumber)}$.
* **Baseline Value:** **98 accounts** (out of 122 registered accounts; 24 inactive prospects).

### 2.2. Repeat Customer Rate
* **Business Definition:** Percentage of purchasing customers who have placed 2 or more distinct orders.
* **Formula:** $\frac{\text{Customers with } \ge 2 \text{ orders}}{\text{Active Purchasing Customers}} \times 100 = \frac{97}{98} \times 100$.
* **Baseline Value:** **98.98%** (97 repeat buyers, 1 single-order customer).

### 2.3. Customer Lifetime Order Value (CLV Proxy)
* **Business Definition:** Cumulative gross realized order value placed by an individual customer account.
* **Formula:** $\sum (\text{quantityOrdered} \times \text{priceEach})$ partitioned by `customerNumber`.
* **Range:** $4,582.47 to $820,689.54 (Top account: Euro+ Shopping Channel).

### 2.4. RFM Segment Distribution
* **Recency:** Days between fixed benchmark cutoff date (`2005-06-01`) and the customer's most recent shipped order date.
* **Frequency:** Distinct count of shipped orders placed by the customer.
* **Monetary:** Total realized shipped revenue placed by the customer.
* **Strategic Segments:**
  1. *Champions* (R $\ge$ 3, F $\ge$ 3, M $\ge$ 3): 24 accounts | $3.59M revenue (40.5% share).
  2. *Loyal Customers* (R $\ge$ 3, F $\ge$ 2): 26 accounts | $1.87M revenue (21.1% share).
  3. *High Value At Risk* (R $\le$ 2, F $\ge$ 2, M $\ge$ 3): 14 accounts | $1.91M revenue (21.6% share).
  4. *Needs Attention* (R = 2): 10 accounts | $489K revenue (5.5% share).
  5. *At Risk* (R = 1, F $\ge$ 2): 9 accounts | $453K revenue (5.1% share).
  6. *New / Potential Loyalists* (R $\ge$ 3, F = 1): 1 account | $46K revenue (0.5% share).
  7. *Hibernating* (R = 1, F = 1, M $\le$ 2): 14 accounts | $506K revenue (5.7% share).
  8. *Prospect / Inactive* (0 orders): 24 accounts | $0 revenue.

## 3. Product Merchandising & Profitability KPIs

### 3.1. Estimated Gross Profit
* **Business Definition:** Realized dollar markup generated by sold merchandise above catalog acquisition cost.
* **Formula:** $\sum \text{quantityOrdered} \times (\text{priceEach} - \text{buyPrice})$.
* **Granularity:** Order-Line Item (`orderdetails`).
* **Baseline Value:** **$3,536,929.17**.
* **Assumptions:** `buyPrice` is a static catalog-level cost benchmark. Inbound ocean freight, tariff duties, warehouse picking costs, and volume supplier rebates are excluded.

### 3.2. Estimated Gross Margin Percentage
* **Business Definition:** Proportion of realized revenue retained after deducting cost of goods sold proxy.
* **Formula:** $\frac{\text{Estimated Gross Profit}}{\text{Total Realized Revenue}} \times 100 = \frac{\$3,536,929.17}{\$8,865,094.64} \times 100$.
* **Baseline Value:** **39.90%**.

### 3.3. ABC Product Classification
* **Class A (Core 80%):** Top 60 SKUs (55.0% of ordered catalog) driving **79.8% of realized revenue ($7.07M)**.
* **Class B (Secondary 15%):** 29 SKUs driving **15.1% of realized revenue ($1.34M)**.
* **Class C (Long-Tail 5%):** 20 SKUs driving **5.1% of realized revenue ($451.7K)**.

### 3.4. Warehouse Stock-to-Demand Ratio
* **Business Definition:** Ratio of current warehouse inventory units to historical units sold over 2.4 years.
* **Formula:** $\frac{\text{products.quantityInStock}}{\sum \text{quantityOrdered}}$.
* **Categories:**
  - *Dead Stock (Zero Demand):* Ratio = $\infty$ (0 historical orders).
  - *Over-Stocked:* Ratio $> 5.0$.
  - *Healthy Stock:* $0.25 \le \text{Ratio} \le 5.0$.
  - *Low Stock Risk:* Ratio $< 0.25$.

## 4. Fulfillment & Operational Operations KPIs

### 4.1. Average Shipping Duration
* **Business Definition:** Mean elapsed calendar days between order placement and warehouse departure.
* **Formula:** $\text{AVG}(\text{orders.shippedDate} - \text{orders.orderDate})$.
* **Baseline Value:** **3.76 calendar days** across 312 orders with recorded departure dates.

### 4.2. Late Shipment Rate (SLA Breach %)
* **Business Definition:** Percentage of fulfilled orders whose departure date exceeded the contracted `requiredDate`.
* **Formula:** $\frac{\text{COUNT}(\text{shippedDate} > \text{requiredDate})}{\text{COUNT}(\text{shipped orders})} \times 100$.
* **Baseline Value:** **0.33%** (1 late shipment out of 303 shipped orders).

## 5. Cash Collections & Treasury KPIs

### 5.1. Total Cash Collections
* **Business Definition:** Aggregate value of customer remittance vouchers received and deposited.
* **Formula:** $\sum \text{payments.amount}$.
* **Baseline Value:** **$8,853,839.23** across 273 payment vouchers.

### 5.2. Net Account Balance Exposure Proxy
* **Business Definition:** Account-level variance between cumulative shipped order billing and cumulative cash collections.
* **Formula:** $\text{Total Realized Shipped Revenue} - \text{Total Cash Collected}$.
* **Portfolio Aggregate:** $\$8,865,094.64 - \$8,853,839.23 = \mathbf{+\$11,255.41}$ net uncollected balance across all active accounts.
* **Limitations:** Not an aged accounts receivable invoice ledger; payments in this schema are unlinked to individual order identifiers.
